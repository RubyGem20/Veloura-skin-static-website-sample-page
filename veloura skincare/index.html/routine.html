THE SKINCARE JOURNAL

How to Build an Easy Morning & Evening Skincare Routine

Taking care of your skin doesn't have to be complicated. A simple and consistent skincare routine can help keep your skin clean, hydrated and healthy-looking.

☀️ Your Morning Routine

Your morning routine should focus on cleansing, hydration and protecting your skin throughout the day.

1. Cleanse

  • Start your morning with a gentle cleanser to remove excess oil and impurities that may have built up overnight.

2. Apply Your Serum

  • Apply a serum that suits your skin's needs. For example, a Vitamin C serum can be used as part of a morning routine to help brighten the appearance of dull skin.

3. Moisturize

  • Follow with a moisturizer to help keep your skin hydrated and comfortable throughout the day.

4. Don't Forget Sunscreen

  • Sunscreen should be the final step of your morning skincare routine. Choose a broad-spectrum sunscreen suitable for your skin.

🌙 Your Evening Routine

Your evening routine gives your skin an opportunity to be cleansed and moisturized before you go to sleep.

1. Cleanse

  • Remove makeup, sunscreen, dirt and excess oil with a gentle cleanser.

2. Apply Treatment Products

  • Apply your chosen treatment products or serums. Avoid using too many active ingredients at once.

3. Moisturize

  • Finish with a moisturizer to help keep your skin hydrated overnight.

✨ Keep It Simple

  • You don't need a shelf full of products to have a good skincare routine.
  • Start with the basics.
  • Remain consistent with your routine.

Cleanse. Treat. Moisturize. Protect.
