THE SKINCARE JOURNAL

Common Skincare Mistakes You Should Avoid

A good skincare routine isn't just about the products you use. It's also about how you use them.

❌ 1. Using Too Many Products

  • It can be tempting to try every new skincare product you see. However, using too many products at once can make it difficult to know what works for your skin.
  • Start with a simple routine and introduce new products gradually.

❌ 2. Skipping Sunscreen

  • Sunscreen is an important part of a daytime skincare routine. Remember to apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day.

❌ 3. Over-Cleansing

  • Washing your face excessively can leave your skin feeling dry or uncomfortable.
  • Use a gentle cleanser and avoid unnecessary washing.

❌ 4. Picking at Your Skin

  • Constantly touching or picking at spots can irritate your skin and may increase the risk of marks.

❌ 5. Changing Products Too Quickly

  • Switching between products every few days makes it difficult to understand how your skin responds to them.

❌ 6. Forgetting to Moisturize

  • Even oily skin needs hydration.
  • Choose a moisturizer that suits your skin type and use it consistently.

✨ The Key Is Consistency

  • Skincare isn't about having the most products.
  • Find a simple routine that works for you.
  • Stay consistent and give your skin time.

Keep it simple. Be consistent. Give your skin time.
