THE SKINCARE JOURNAL

How to Layer Your Skincare Products

Have you ever wondered which skincare product should go first? The order in which you apply your products can make your routine easier and more effective.

1. Start With Clean Skin

  • Begin with a gentle cleanser. This removes dirt, excess oil and other impurities from your skin.

2. Apply Toner

  • If toner is part of your routine, apply it after cleansing. Choose a toner based on what your skin needs.

3. Apply Your Serum

  • Serums are generally lightweight and contain concentrated ingredients. Apply your serum after cleansing and toning.

4. Moisturize

  • Apply your moisturizer after your serum. Moisturizer helps keep your skin hydrated and comfortable.

5. Sunscreen in the Morning

  • During the day, sunscreen should generally be the final step of your skincare routine.

💡 A Simple Rule

  • A useful general rule is to apply products from the thinnest texture to the thickest.
  • Some products have specific instructions, so always follow the directions provided with your skincare products.

✨ Don't Overcomplicate Your Routine

  • More products don't necessarily mean better skin.
  • Choose products that work well together.
  • Introduce new products gradually.
